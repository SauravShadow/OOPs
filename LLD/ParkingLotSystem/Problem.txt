Design a parking lot system than can handle multiple floors, different vehicle types(bike, car, truck),
and keeps track of entry, exit, and available parking spots efficiently.

Support:
- Assigning a parking spot to a vehicle when it enters.
- Releasing a spot when it exits.
- Display available spots by type and floor.

Assumption:
- Each floor has limited slots for each vehicle type.
- Vehicle gets the nearest available spots of their type.


Summary:
- The parking lot has 3 floors.
- Each floor has different sections for each vehicle type:
    - 100 -> Bike spots
    - 200 -> Car spots
    - 50 -> Truck spots
- There are 2 entry and 2 exits point
- When assigning spots, need to assign an unique spot for each entry
    - We can use multi-threading to do assign the spot
    - The spots can be stored in the min-heap (priority queue).
    - Let's say the spots are number from nearest to end by 1- 100 : -> 1 means the nearest and 100 the farthest.
    - Whenever the spots are freed it can be inserted back.
- Billing system - Time based and different for each vehicle type.
- Vehicle type - BIKE, CAR, TRUCK.
