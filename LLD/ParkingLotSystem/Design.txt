Let's follow top-bottom approach

A vehicle comes near one entry gate and is at the billing place.
At billing place, his vehicle is identified, he is given the entry time and the spot number.
He goes and parks it there.
After some time he comes back take his vehicle goes near the exit route.
His time is noted, the bill is generated and the bill is paid.
